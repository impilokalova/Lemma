# Creator milestone · 100K
**Lemma Studio · Fictional portfolio case study**

## The brief
Independent creator growing a video-first community.

## The challenge
Turn 100K into a multi-platform celebration with a memorable visual system.

## Creative approach
A milestone announcement that feels like an event, not a screenshot.

## Design direction
Gold and coral against near-black; play-button motif; oversized numerical milestone.

## Campaign system
Poster, YouTube thumbnail, story countdown, 3-slide carousel, community thank-you card.

## Storytelling strategy
Celebrate the audience, spotlight the creator, then invite the next chapter.

## Intended business outcome and measurement
Example KPIs to track after a real campaign: save rate, shares, returning viewers and audience comments.

## Integrity statement
This is a **self-initiated concept**, not a completed paid client project. No campaign outcomes, revenue or reach are claimed. The SVG poster is a fully editable vector sample and the additional deliverables are an illustrative proposed scope rather than completed production assets.
