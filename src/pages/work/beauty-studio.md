# Beauty studio launch
**Lemma Studio · Fictional portfolio case study**

## The brief
Fictional appointment-based beauty studio.

## The challenge
Introduce a distinctive service identity and encourage enquiries without hard-sell clutter.

## Creative approach
An elegant launch campaign built for premium local services.

## Design direction
Muted rose, cream, soft editorial typography, restrained portrait line art.

## Campaign system
Launch poster, service-menu sheet, story set, appointment reminder and window graphic.

## Storytelling strategy
Introduce the ritual, explain services, show social proof, simplify booking.

## Intended business outcome and measurement
Example KPIs: appointment enquiries, click-to-book rate, repeat visits.

## Integrity statement
This is a **self-initiated concept**, not a completed paid client project. No campaign outcomes, revenue or reach are claimed. The SVG poster is a fully editable vector sample and the additional deliverables are an illustrative proposed scope rather than completed production assets.
