# Property showcase
**Lemma Studio · Fictional portfolio case study**

## The brief
Fictional residential listing and local estate marketing team.

## The challenge
Turn listing specifications into a coherent visual story for buyers and renters.

## Creative approach
A modern property marketing system that makes space feel aspirational.

## Design direction
Deep teal, warm metallic accents, architectural geometry and strong grid.

## Campaign system
Listing hero poster, brochure cover, social carousel, neighbourhood map and virtual-tour teaser.

## Storytelling strategy
Lead with lifestyle, support with facts, end with an obvious viewing action.

## Intended business outcome and measurement
Example KPIs: listing saves, qualified enquiries, viewing bookings.

## Integrity statement
This is a **self-initiated concept**, not a completed paid client project. No campaign outcomes, revenue or reach are claimed. The SVG poster is a fully editable vector sample and the additional deliverables are an illustrative proposed scope rather than completed production assets.
