# Six seasonal landing page demos

Routes: `/work/demos` and `/work/demos/:slug`.

All six pages are fictional portfolio concepts, not real discounts, shops, tickets or clients. Their images are the user's existing uploaded PNGs in `src/pages/packages/`; no remote stock dependencies. The reusable registry and pages live in `SeasonalDemos.tsx`, with styles in `seasonal.css`. Figma concept boards: https://www.figma.com/design/aT6Tr0QoWQBtkapEWPpUvC.
