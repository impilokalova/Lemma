# Creative direction / six case studies
Existing portfolio concepts (keep unchanged):
1. After Dark — Halloween campaign
2. Fire & Form — restaurant menu
3. Sneaker Drop — retail Black Friday

New concepts (included in this ZIP):
4. The 100K Moment — creator milestone
5. Soft Power — beauty studio campaign
6. Live Beyond — property showcase

## Why these three?
Creator milestone: video-first creators need recognisable milestone assets, thumbnails and audience celebrations.
Beauty studio: high-frequency visual promotions, service menus, social stories and local appointment acquisition.
Property showcase: high-value listings require strong imagery, brochures, maps and enquiry journeys.

## Design distinction
These are self-initiated original concept studies. No brand is a real client. Do not invent customer outcomes.
